About me

I'm a network technician student and a co-founder who takes ideas all the way to a finished product.

I study computer networks in Bratislava on the CCNA track, and I co-founded AlmiBoost, a B2B startup selling NFC-enabled Google Review cards to local businesses.

At AlmiBoost I own the full stack: sourcing NFC hardware directly from manufacturers, building the website, running in-person sales and keeping a structured pitch-tracking system. I've learned first-hand what it takes to go from an idea to something a customer can tap their phone against.

On the side I build hardware with Arduino and NFC, 3D print on a Prusa MINI, make automation tools like a Rust and Tauri video pipeline, and experiment with local LLM tooling through Ollama.

I'd like to connect with people in networking, B2B sales, hardware and IoT, and early-stage startups.
